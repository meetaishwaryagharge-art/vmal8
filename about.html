The Vmal Clean-Water Advantage

What Makes Vmal Different?

The answer lies in our science. The vast majority of liquid cleaning products on the market use standard municipal or borewell water, which introduces heavy minerals that weaken active cleaning enzymes.

At our modern manufacturing plant, we have eliminated this flaw completely. Every bottle of Vmal is formulated using water processed through our industrial-grade, multi-stage Reverse Osmosis (RO) Purification System. By stripping the water base down to its purest form, we ensure that our active cleaning agents remain exceptionally stable, dissolve grease effortlessly, lather beautifully, and extend product shelf life.
